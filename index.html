League standings

Each qualifying weekend: best 2 of 3 reported 12-hole net Stableford cards; absent cards score zero. Points are used unchanged, with no rescaling to 18 holes or handicap conversions. Live tables have no discards. Committee assumption: tied retained totals compare discarded scores highest first; remaining ties share rank, not alphabetical resolution.

Singles leaderboard

Individual reported 12-hole net Stableford cards for one weekend, not a cumulative singles championship. All imported submitted cards count, including zero, regardless of team registration. No team best-two selection or discards apply. Equal points share competition rank (1, 1, 3). Official singles countback is not available from the imported numeric fields; alphabetical order within ties is display only, not a tie-break. Canceled and excluded weekends are available for historical review only.

Registered teams

Weekend schedule

Saturday is the weekend key, including Sunday cards. Weather cancellation excludes the entire weekend for every team.

Individual weekend cards

All three players' reported 12-hole net Stableford cards are retained and shown unchanged. A team's weekly score uses only its highest two cards; the third card is not deleted.

Unassigned cards

Scores can arrive before registration. They remain unassigned until a matching player is registered.

Winter League final

Zero league carryover. Final date awaiting committee confirmation unless a confirmed date is entered.

All 3 distinct registered players must submit final cards before their team is ranked. Countback aggregates all 3 players' back 9, 6, 3 and 1 in that order. Unresolved ties share rank.

Enter final cards through Administration. Template scores are blank, not invented results.

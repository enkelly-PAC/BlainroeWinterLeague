Unassigned cards

Scoring details and report

Reported 12-hole net Stableford points are used unchanged, with no rescaling or handicap conversions. Individual 0 means a submitted zero. Missing and held cards contribute zero to the best-two calculation, but are not displayed as submitted zeros. Total is cumulative after any discards; D marks an excluded team weekend score. Cancelled and excluded weekends remain in Team list and weekends. Full round titles are available on the round headings. Live tables have no discards. Committee assumption: tied retained totals compare discarded scores highest first; remaining ties share rank.

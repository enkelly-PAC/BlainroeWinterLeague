Scoring details and report

Reported 12-hole net Stableford points are used unchanged, with no rescaling or handicap conversions. Individual 0 means a submitted zero. Missing and held cards contribute zero to the best-two calculation, but are not displayed as submitted zeros. Total is cumulative after any discards; D marks an excluded team weekend score. Cancelled and excluded weekends remain in Team list and weekends. Full round titles are available on the round headings. Live tables have no discards. Committee assumption: tied retained totals compare discarded scores highest first; remaining ties share rank.

Upload and review results

Singles leaderboard

Individual reported 12-hole net Stableford cards for one weekend, not a cumulative singles championship. All imported submitted cards count, including zero, regardless of team registration. No team best-two selection or discards apply. Equal points share competition rank (1, 1, 3). Official singles countback is not available from the imported numeric fields; alphabetical order within ties is display only, not a tie-break. Canceled and excluded weekends are available for historical review only.

Individual weekend cards

All three players' reported 12-hole net Stableford cards are retained and shown unchanged. A team's weekly score uses only its highest two cards; the third card is not deleted.

Unassigned cards

Scores can arrive before registration. They remain unassigned until a matching player is registered.

Winter League final

Zero league carryover. Final date awaiting committee confirmation unless a confirmed date is entered.

All 3 distinct registered players must submit final cards before their team is ranked. Countback aggregates all 3 players' back 9, 6, 3 and 1 in that order. Unresolved ties share rank.

Enter final cards through the leaderboard's Upload and review results, then Other imports. Template scores are blank, not invented results.

Include them with your net results in the same selection. Recognized Competition Scorecards contain hole-by-hole gross data: they are excluded from League and Singles net scoring and are not stored. Edit team list

Add or change teams here without editing source files. New teams need three players; Team 34 may keep its vacant third place. Existing team numbers stay fixed.

Changing a player changes team membership for all weekends. Raw cards are never renamed or deleted. Spelling, accents, punctuation and surname-first names match automatically; other changes can leave old cards unassigned. Held names remain in Singles but contribute zero to team scoring until resolved. Notes record eligibility questions, not eligibility approval or automatic score exclusion. Review unassigned cards and back up first.
